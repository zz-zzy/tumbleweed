> **TL;DR** — Readers want the conclusion first, then the evidence. Verifiable judgment beats polished prose.

## Structure

1. **TL;DR** — three lines, conclusion plus scope.
2. **Context** — why this problem matters.
3. **Method and trade-offs** — what you tried and why you chose this.
4. **Limits** — when it stops working.
5. **References** — primary sources first.

## A formula

$$
S = \sum_{i=1}^{n} w_i \cdot s_i, \quad \sum_{i=1}^{n} w_i = 1
$$

The formula is not the point; the weights should come from business goals, not from what is easy to compute.

## Checklist

| Item | Question |
|---|---|
| Conclusion | Can a reader act after the first paragraph? |
| Evidence | Is every claim backed by data, code or a citation? |
| Limits | Are the non-applicable cases stated? |
